About Crystal

An architect building more than buildings

Crystal Kizor is the Design Director of Studio COKA and the founder of a growing family of initiatives in design, education and youth development.

Her work combines modern practice with contextual design to improve how people live. Shade, airflow, light and local materials come first, so buildings stay comfortable without relying on machines. That same thinking shapes everything else she builds.

"We begin with a simple question: how were humans originally meant to live?"
